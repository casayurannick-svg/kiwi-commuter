```
Active: <KC-NNN, branch, state> or none.
Just shipped: <KC-NNN: one line each>.
Next: <KC-NNN> (then <KC-NNN>).
Owner actions pending: <dashboard / SQL steps not yet done>.
Open questions: <decisions waiting on the owner>.

```
